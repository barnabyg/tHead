# Conversation avatars

Named conversation partners combine a portrait with local English speech.

## Language

**Avatar**: A conversation partner with a stable identity, a display name, a portrait and a voice. Multiple avatars may share a voice.
_Avoid_: Pair, portrait/voice identity

**Avatar name**: The user-facing label for an avatar, such as Grace or JFK. A name may contain spaces and is independent of its identity and properties.

**Portrait**: The still image used to represent an avatar and animate its spoken responses.

**Voice**: A supported local English speech choice, independent of the avatar using it.
